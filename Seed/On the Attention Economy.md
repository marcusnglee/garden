       The price of attention, I believe, will soon be correct4ed

Take the cases of **Clavicular** and **Roy Lee**, people who have traded in personal reputation and morality for attention. 
*note: reference youtube video here*

It seems increasingly that my generation (gen-z) has lost faith in institutions and systems of which we were told will provide as long as we play within it. (find the twitlonger about the house and gambling) There seems to be a rejection of attempting any kind of moral good in your work, and instead amass as much as capital for yourself. Perhaps this is the natural waves of generations, in which we are similar to baby boomers while millenials were closer to the flower power generation.

However, the proliferation of AI combined with this loss of trust in institutions actually makes reputation direly important. When people lose trust in the system, who will they turn to for recommendations, opinions, and more? They go to substack, influencers with reputation, to seek more reputable forms of knowledge and opinion. 

Individuals are getting much more leverage. Take ASAP Rocky and his partnerships with Rayban for example. Rocky doesn’t need Rayban. **Rayban needs Rocky.** Institutions are no longer legitimizing individuals, but rather the other way around.

Reputation, influence, street cred, style, taste, whatever you want to call it. It becomes direly important.

Roy Lee has traded in his reputation and what he maybe *actually* stands for, in order to market himself as degenarate, alternative, and outside the system. I’m happy to eat my words here later in the future, but once Cluely runs its course and Roy Lee loses his leverage point for attention, no one will be willing to engage. After the meming is done, what will you have left? The same goes for clavicular

When I think about what I choose to believe in for myself, often it boils down to this:
***Who benefits from me choosing to believe this?***

It’s clear as day who benefits from this loss of hope and trust in morality: casinos, prediction markets. I’m from Vegas. The house always wins. You’re attempt to circumvent the system through degeneracy is not escaping the matrix. Its simply the same house with a new skin.

I don’t want a good reputation amongst 100,000s. If this is the case, I’ve likely lost my authenticity. I want a specific, stylized reputation amongst maybe a few hundred. Diving deeper, I believe more in genuine, analog, private connection. Something only the two participating parties benefit from, perhaps.
